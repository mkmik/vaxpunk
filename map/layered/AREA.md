What a VMS site bought on top of the operating system to run its
business: the transaction processing stack (the ACMS and DECintact TP
monitors, DECforms, FMS and TDMS for screens, CDD for record
definitions, Rdb and DBMS for databases, DATATRIEVE for queries, RTR and
DECmessageQ between systems), DECset's programming tools (CMS, MMS,
LSE, SCA, PCA, DTM), office and publishing (ALL-IN-1, VAX Notes, VAX
DOCUMENT), and system management (Volume Shadowing, Availability
Manager, POLYCENTER, DECprint Supervisor, DQS, backup managers).
Languages and DECwindows are layered products too, but have areas of
their own.

None of it is built, and all of it is aspiration. One thread is Marko's
own: a home-office inventory as a "signature application" for a world
where VMS is alive, on ACMS, DECforms, CDD and RMS indexed files, with
Rdb left out. Its chain runs through [ACMS](acms.md) and
[DECforms](decforms.md) down to mailboxes, the lock manager and DECdtm
in the executive.
